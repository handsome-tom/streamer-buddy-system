Privacy policy

For the StreamerSpace Chrome extension and Streamer Buddy System. Last updated November 8, 2026.

Short version: StreamerSpace keeps what you save inside your own browser. It doesn't sell anything, show ads, or track you. The only things that leave your browser are the Streamer Buddy System messages you choose to send to people you've connected with.

What stays in your browser

  • Your settings, quick phrases, highlight words, chatter notes, pinned emotes and channels, favorite rewards, watch streaks and video markers. These are kept in Chrome's storage for the extension, and a copy is kept in twitch.tv's own storage in your browser so they survive a reinstall.
  • Account switcher: if you use it, the extension saves the Twitch sign-in cookies of each account you add, so it can switch between them. They're stored only in your browser and only used to sign you back into twitch.tv. They are never sent anywhere else and are left out of backups.
  • Backups you make with the Back up button are files on your own computer.

What is sent, and where

  • Twitch. The extension works on twitch.tv and talks to Twitch the same way the Twitch website does, as you.
  • Streamer Buddy System (optional). If you sign in with Twitch in the side panel, your Twitch username and a sign-in token go to the Streamer Buddy System relay (a small server on Cloudflare run by the developer). The relay keeps the list of people you've connected with, and passes your questions, answers, notes and flagged chat lines to them. Messages are deleted after a week at most. If a streamer turns on stream stats sharing, daily viewer numbers are kept for 40 days and monthly totals for 13 months. Sign-in asks Twitch for your channel's mods and the channels you mod, to fill the "Your mods" and "Channels I mod" lists. Nothing else is requested.
  • The side panel loads its page from handsome-tom.github.io, where Streamer Buddy System is hosted.

What isn't collected

  • No analytics, no ads, no tracking, and no browsing history outside twitch.tv.
  • Nothing is sold or shared with anyone other than the people you message through Streamer Buddy System.

Removing your data

  • Removing the extension deletes what it saved in Chrome. Clearing twitch.tv's site data in Chrome removes the copy kept there.
  • To have your Streamer Buddy System connections and messages removed from the relay, open an issue at the link below.
