Privacy policy

For the StreamerSpace Chrome extension and Streamer Buddy System. Last updated November 12, 2026.

Short version: StreamerSpace keeps what you save inside your own browser. It doesn't sell anything, show ads, or track you. The only things that leave your browser are for Streamer Buddy System, if you sign in to it (your connections and the messages you choose to send to them), and the names of channels whose public details a feature looks up.

What stays in your browser

  • Your settings, quick phrases, highlight words, chatter notes, pinned emotes and channels, favorite rewards, watch streaks and video markers. These are kept in Chrome's storage for the extension, and a copy is kept in twitch.tv's own storage in your browser so they survive a reinstall.
  • Account switcher: if you use it, the extension saves the Twitch sign-in cookies of each account you add, so it can switch between them. They're stored only in your browser and only used to sign you back into twitch.tv. They are never sent anywhere else and are left out of backups.
  • Backups you make with the Back up button are files on your own computer.

What is sent, and where

  • Twitch. The extension works on twitch.tv and talks to Twitch the same way the Twitch website does, as you.
  • Public channel lookups. A few features need public facts from Twitch: pinned channels (pictures, who's live, viewer counts, when they were last live), your chat name color, and when a stream or video started, for video markers. These are asked through the Streamer Buddy System relay, which asks Twitch's official API. Only the channel names or video number go with the request, never your sign-in, and Cloudflare sees your IP address as with any website. The relay keeps the answers in memory for up to an hour so it doesn't ask Twitch again, and doesn't log who asked.
  • Streamer Buddy System (optional). If you sign in with Twitch in the side panel, your Twitch username and a sign-in token go to the Streamer Buddy System relay (a small server on Cloudflare run by the developer). The relay keeps the list of people you've connected with, and passes your questions, answers, notes and flagged chat lines to them. Messages are deleted after a week at most. Chat notes (a note a mod leaves on a line of a channel's chat, live or on a VOD) are kept with that moment of the stream for up to 60 days, so they show on the VOD, and only the channel's streamer and their mutual connections can see them. If a streamer turns on stream stats sharing, daily viewer numbers are kept for 40 days and monthly totals for 13 months. Sign-in asks Twitch for your channel's mods and the channels you mod, to fill the "Your mods" and "Channels I mod" lists. Nothing else is requested.
  • Who can see your messages. Messages are encrypted on their way to and from the relay, but they aren't end-to-end encrypted. While they're kept on the relay, the developer who runs it (and Cloudflare, which hosts it) could technically see them. They aren't read, used or shared for anything except passing them on, unless the law requires it (see below).
  • The side panel loads its page from handsome-tom.github.io, where Streamer Buddy System is hosted.

What isn't collected

  • No analytics, no ads, no tracking, and no browsing history outside twitch.tv.
  • Nothing is sold or shared with anyone other than the people you message through Streamer Buddy System.

Legal requests

If a valid legal order requires it, what the relay holds at that moment may have to be handed over. Because messages are deleted after a week, there usually isn't much to give.

Age

StreamerSpace and Streamer Buddy System are for people 13 and older, the same as Twitch. Don't use them if you're younger. If you think someone under 13 has signed in to Streamer Buddy System, email the address below and their data will be removed.

Removing your data

  • Removing the extension deletes what it saved in Chrome. Clearing twitch.tv's site data in Chrome removes the copy kept there.
  • To have your Streamer Buddy System connections and messages removed from the relay, email the address below. You don't have to give a reason, and the request stays private.
