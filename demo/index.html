What it does

Streamer Buddy System lets mods look out for the streamer and ask quick things, without cluttering the stream. Mods use a side panel in Chrome (part of the Twitch Space extension); the streamer adds one dock to OBS, which is also their chat.

For mods: the side panel

Opens beside Twitch, from the handshake button.

Streamer View
What the streamer's OBS dock is showing right now: its glow colour, everything waiting there (from anyone), answers not yet seen, and the notes on their bar. It follows the channel you're watching. Your own things there have an ×, which takes them back off the streamer's dock if they haven't been answered or seen yet.
Ask, Quick info or !vanish
One typing bar: pick the kind, type, press Enter. Questions get a one-tap answer: ♥, ✓, ✗ or ? (maybe), or a typed reply. Quick info shows on the streamer's bar for 2 minutes; a !vanish note for 30 seconds.
Nudge
Every question arrives green. If it isn't answered, Nudge moves it up to orange, then yellow, then white, with one soft beep each. At most three, 15 seconds apart, and nothing climbs by itself.
Send a warning
When something needs the streamer right now, tap Nudge six times quickly. It turns red on the streamer's screen with a louder double beep. At most one every 30 seconds.
Your lists
Newest first, one line each, with small answer buttons, a reply box and ×. Clear all puts everything away at once.
Connections and private chat
Everyone you've added, with their picture. Click someone for a chat only the two of you see. Tags sit on the right.
Status
Online, Idle (away for a bit) or Offline. Things still reach you either way.
Flag a chat line
Point at a line in Twitch's chat and click the flag. It goes to the streamer with who said it and who flagged it, and glows in the chat of everyone connected with the streamer.

For the streamer: the OBS dock

One panel in OBS, with nothing on stream.

The message area
What's waiting shows just under the bar, in a soft glow coloured by how urgent it is. Answer with one tap (♥ ✓ ✗ ?) or type a reply; × dismisses it. With several, they take turns, and ‹ › flips through them.
The glow
Green when something arrives; orange, yellow and white as a mod nudges it; red for a warning. It glows for a moment, then rests, and only comes back while it's unanswered.
Quick info and !vanish
Full width, like a question: the lightning bolt for quick info (2 minutes), the little devil for !vanish (30 seconds).
Who's in chat
Each mod's picture in the bar, ringed by how recently they chatted, and grey once they've left. The small dot in the corner shows the connection is working.
Do Not Disturb
The hand: for 15 minutes or until it's turned off. No sounds, no glow, no message card, and no quick info or !vanish notes; everything waits and shows once it's over. Mods see "Do Not Disturb" in Streamer View.
Back to chat
The chat bubble closes whatever's open and goes straight back to chat.
Asking your mods
Type in the bar's Message field and press Enter; the picker beside it starts with the last person you talked to.
Settings
Sound, volume, reminder beeps, edge glow, the quick-questions row, custom or Twitch chat, chat text size, and whether mods see Streamer View.

Custom chat

The streamer's chat, inside the dock, with Twitch's own features and a few of its own.

Hover a message
Bookmark it for later, pin it to the top of chat (for how long you pick), or reply. Click a message to draw a "where I was" line under it.
Mod icons
Before each name: ban, time out, warn and delete, all real actions on Twitch. Settings can hide them.
Name card
Click a name for Twitch's info: picture, when the account was made, following since, sub tier and months, badges, and Gift a Sub, Follow, Whisper and the mod actions.
Raids
A card with the raiding streamer's picture and viewer count, in their name colour, and a Welcome button. People who came with the raid get a RAID tag with that streamer's small picture for 15 minutes. Twitch doesn't tell apps who the raiders are, so this is a careful guess: new in chat since the raid, and not here before.
First-time chatters
A green "First-time chatter" label, just like Twitch.
Subs, gifts, streaks and bits
Cards with a big number: months subscribed, watch streaks, bits badges and cheers. A gift bomb lists everyone who got a sub.
Channel points
Highlighted messages stand out, and redemptions show who redeemed what. Every redemption shows on the streamer's own channel; elsewhere, only ones with a message.
Top of chat
The pinned message, gift progress, hype train, polls and predictions, live, each with its own card. Twitch shares the hype train, polls and predictions only with the streamer, and the pinned message with the streamer and mods.
Announcements and bots
Announcements get their own card, and bot replies (like StreamElements commands) show as normal chat.
One colour per kind
Subs purple, gifts pink, streaks orange, bits teal, channel points blue, announcements amber, first-time chatters green, so you know what it is at a glance.
Sharp emotes
Twitch, 7TV, BTTV and FFZ emotes and badges load crisp copies on high-resolution screens.

Good to know

Privacy, and this demo.

Who sees what
Questions, answers and flagged lines go only to the people you've connected with. Nothing shows on stream.
It's all real Twitch data
In the real thing, chat, names, badges, events and mod actions come straight from Twitch.
This demo
Everyone here is pretend and nothing is sent anywhere. "mod2" answers whatever you send a few seconds later, and a "minute" for the pictures' rings is 6 seconds. Start over resets everything.
