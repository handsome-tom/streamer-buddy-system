StreamSpace · your people, one small space

Your little corner of Twitch, supported by your community.MySpace 2.0

Not a social network, just a small hub for the people who make a stream feel like home. Whoever's live, whoever's modding and whoever's just hanging out in chat, you're all part of it.

For mods

Back up your homies, behind the scenes.

A side panel next to Twitch for asking, nudging and looking out, kept between you, the streamer and the mods you're connected with. Chat stays clean, and nobody has to go it alone.

  • Ask without cluttering chat

    Ask, Quick info or a !vanish note: type it, press Enter. The streamer answers with one tap, and nothing ever shows on stream.

  • Mod tools, one click away

    Refund a channel points redemption, flip slow or emote-only mode, or line up a raid that goes only when the streamer taps Raid.

  • Nudge, or hit the panic button

    Nudge a question that's waiting. If it can't wait, the panic button flashes the streamer's dock red with an alarm. Limited, so it never turns into spam.

  • Flag a line, or leave a note on it

    Flag a message so it glows in the streamer's chat, or pin a note to it that only your circle can see.

  • The Cat Signal

    When a channel you mod for goes live, a green cat starts spinning on the StreamSpace icon.

  • Private chats and mod rooms

    Talk one-to-one, or in a group room like “Mod squad,” with the streamer and the mods you're connected with.

  • See what they see

    Streamer View shows exactly what's on the streamer's dock right now, so you never ask twice or pile on.

For the streamer

Keep yapping without breaking the flow.

Chat, your mods and the Twitch bits that matter, in one OBS dock that stays quiet until someone actually needs you. Your friends keep an eye out so you can just stream.

  • Chat and your mods, one dock

    Your chat, Twitch's events and your mods' questions in one OBS dock. Your mods' pictures sit in the bar, ringed when they've just chatted.

  • Answer in one tap

    A mod's question pops into your chat like a text from a friend and sticks to the top until you answer with ♥ ✓ ✗ or ?, or type a quick reply.

  • Only loud when it matters

    A steady outline while something's waiting, warmer each time a mod nudges it, and flashing red with an alarm only for the panic button, with a little Stop to hush it in one click.

  • Moderate right from chat

    Hover any line to delete it, time someone out, ban or warn them, without opening Twitch's mod view.

  • Know who's raiding

    The raid card shows who it is and how many came, whether they follow you, and if they've raided or chatted here before.

  • Do Not Disturb

    Deep in a boss fight? One click and everything waits silently until you're ready.

  • Chat that waits for you

    Scroll up and chat holds still, counting what's new. Bookmark a line or drop a “where I was” marker and pick up after the fight.

  • Find anything in chat

    Type a name or a few words and only the matching lines show. Pick someone to see their last 50 messages.

For viewers

Just watching? You're part of it too.

The regulars, the lurkers and the people who show up every night are what make a community. This is the rest of StreamSpace: small, friendly extras for anyone who hangs out on Twitch, mod or not.

  • A better player

    Click anywhere on the video to pause, even over overlays like Tangia, with a 1% volume step and a bigger, easier-to-grab playhead on VODs.

  • Two streams at once

    Hover a channel in the sidebar and pick Split screen: two streams side by side, each with its own chat.

  • Watch streaks, kept alive

    A flame shows your streak and how much more to watch for it to count, and a saver quietly rescues one that's at risk.

  • Channel points, sorted

    Search a channel's rewards, star favorites to the top, and bonus points get claimed for you.

  • Notes on people

    Right-click a name to note where you know them from, their pronouns and anything else, so familiar faces never feel like strangers.

  • Bookmarks on streams and VODs

    Right-click the seek bar to drop a marker with a note, live or on a VOD, and jump straight back to it later.

  • Emotes and phrases at hand

    Pin favorite emotes, jump to the channels whose emotes you use most, and keep a list of phrases you type a lot.

  • Your Twitch, tidied

    Pin channels to the sidebar, switch accounts, clear all notifications, quiet the top bar, and back up your settings.

  • Your name stands out

    Your username gets a box in your own chat color wherever it shows up, @ or not, and so do any highlight words you pick.
