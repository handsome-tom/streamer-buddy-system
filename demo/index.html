For mods

Have the streamer's back, quietly.

A side panel next to Twitch for asking, nudging and looking out, kept between you, the streamer and the mods you're connected with. Chat stays clean, and nobody has to go it alone.

  • See what they see

    Streamer View shows exactly what's on the streamer's dock right now, so you never ask twice or pile on.

  • Ask without cluttering chat

    Ask, Quick info or a !vanish note: type it, press Enter. The streamer answers with one tap, and nothing ever shows on stream.

  • Nudge, or hit the panic button

    Nudge a question that's waiting. If it can't wait, the panic button flashes the streamer's dock red with an alarm. Limited, so it never turns into spam.

  • Flag a line, or leave a note on it

    Flag a message so it glows in the streamer's chat, or pin a note to it that only your circle can see.

  • Private chats and mod rooms

    Talk one-to-one, or in a group room like “Mod squad,” with the streamer and the mods you're connected with.

  • Know the moment they go live

    A green dot on the StreamSpace icon whenever a channel you mod for is live. Hover it to see who.

  • A deck on your phone

    Add it to your home screen: big buttons that send a question, quick info or a !vanish note in one tap.

  • Say when you're away

    Set yourself to Online, Idle or Offline, so the streamer knows who's around to help.

For the streamer

Keep yapping. Your people have you covered.

Chat, your mods and the Twitch bits that matter, in one OBS dock that stays quiet until someone actually needs you. Your friends keep an eye out so you can just stream.

  • Chat and your mods, one dock

    Your chat, Twitch's events and your mods' questions together in one OBS dock, so there's one place to look.

  • Answer in one tap

    Questions wait quietly under the bar. Answer with ♥ ✓ ✗ or ?, or type a quick reply, without leaving the conversation.

  • Only loud when it matters

    A steady outline while something's waiting, warmer each time a mod nudges it, and flashing red with an alarm only for the panic button.

  • Every event at a glance

    Subs, gifts, raids, first-time chatters, polls and hype trains arrive as clean color-coded cards, and raiders get a RAID tag.

  • Chat that waits for you

    Scroll up and chat holds still, counting what's new. Bookmark a line or drop a “where I was” marker and pick up after the fight.

  • Find anything in chat

    Type a name or a few words and only the matching lines show. Pick someone to see their last 50 messages.

  • Mod tools right in chat

    Hover any line to delete it, time someone out, ban or warn them, without opening Twitch's mod view.

  • Do Not Disturb

    Deep in a boss fight? One click and everything waits silently until you're ready.

  • See who's around

    Your mods' pictures sit in the bar, ringed when they've just chatted and grayed out when they step away.

For viewers

Just watching? You're part of it too.

The regulars, the lurkers and the people who show up every night are what make a community. This is the rest of StreamSpace: small, friendly extras for anyone who hangs out on Twitch, mod or not.

  • A better player

    Click anywhere to pause, even over overlays like Tangia, with a 1% volume step and easier-to-grab controls.

  • Two streams at once

    Watch two streams side by side, each with its own chat, and pick which one you hear.

  • Watch streaks, kept alive

    A flame shows your streak's progress, and a saver keeps it going quietly in the background.

  • Channel points, sorted

    Search rewards, star favorites to the top, and claim bonus points automatically.

  • Notes on people

    Remember where you know someone from, their pronouns and a note, so familiar faces never feel like strangers in any channel's chat.

  • Bookmarks on streams and VODs

    Mark a moment live or on a VOD, add a note, and jump straight back to it later.

  • Emotes and phrases at hand

    Pin favorite emotes, keep a row of channels you visit, and save phrases you type a lot.

  • Your Twitch, tidied

    Pin channels to the sidebar, switch accounts, clear notifications, and back up all your settings.
