Twitch Space · Streamer Buddy System

Look out for your streamer, without getting in the way.

Twitch Space is a set of small upgrades for Twitch. At its heart is Streamer Buddy System: mods quietly ask the streamer things and keep an eye out for them from a side panel in Chrome, while the streamer sees it all in one calm OBS dock that's also their chat.

Streamers

Keep yapping. We'll keep track.

Your chat, your mods and the Twitch bits you care about, in one OBS dock that's easy to read and stays quiet until something actually needs you. Talk to chat, bookmark what you want to come back to, and let everything else take care of itself.

  • Never lose your place

    Bookmark a message to come back to after the fight, or click one to drop a "where I was" line. Chat keeps moving; you pick up right where you left off.

  • The good stuff, minus the noise

    Subs, gifts, raids, first-time chatters, polls and hype trains land as clean little cards, one color each, so you see what happened at a glance and get back to talking.

  • Your mods, one tap away

    Questions from mods wait quietly under the bar. Answer with ♥ ✓ ✗ or ?, or type a quick reply, without ever leaving the conversation.

  • Only loud when it matters

    A soft glow when something comes in, warmer if a mod nudges it, red only for a real warning. Otherwise it stays out of your way.

  • Heads-ups that clean up after themselves

    "Ad break in 2 min." "You've got this." Quick notes from mods show for a moment and then disappear on their own.

  • Do Not Disturb

    Deep in a boss fight? One click and everything waits, silently, until you're ready.

  • Use the buddy system

    Your mods' pictures sit in the bar while they're in chat, and gray out when they step away.

For mods

Help the streamer without cluttering their chat.

A side panel next to Twitch for asking, nudging and looking out, all kept between you and the streamer.

  • Streamer View

    See exactly what is on the streamer's screen right now, so you never ask twice or pile on.

  • Ask in one line

    Pick Ask, Quick info or !vanish, type, press Enter. The streamer answers with one tap.

  • Nudge and warn

    Nudge a question that's waiting, or send a warning when something can't wait. Limited, so it never turns into spam.

  • Flag a chat line

    Point at a message in Twitch chat and flag it. It glows in the streamer's chat and every connected mod's.

  • Online, Idle or Offline

    Let the streamer know when you're stepping away, so they can leave you be.

  • Private chats

    Chat one-to-one with the streamer or any mod you're connected with, with tags to keep track of who's who.

Quality of life

Twitch, just a bit nicer to use.

The rest of Twitch Space: small fixes and extras for anyone who watches a lot of Twitch.

  • A better player

    Click anywhere to pause, even over overlays like Tangia, a 1% volume button, and easier-to-grab controls.

  • Emotes and quick phrases

    Pin favorite emotes, keep a row of channels you use, and save phrases you type a lot.

  • Watch streaks, saved

    A streak flame with your progress, and a saver that keeps a streak alive quietly in the background.

  • Channel points

    Search rewards, star favorites to the top, and claim bonus points automatically.

  • Notes on people

    Remember where you know someone from, their pronouns and a note, in every channel's chat.

  • Bookmarks and markers

    Mark moments live or on VODs, add notes, and jump back to them later.

  • Split screen

    Watch two streams side by side, each with its own chat, and pick which one you hear.

  • Pinned channels and more

    Pin channels to the sidebar, switch Twitch accounts, clear notifications, and back up everything.

Questions, answers and flagged lines go only to the people you've connected with. In this demo everyone is pretend and nothing is sent anywhere.
