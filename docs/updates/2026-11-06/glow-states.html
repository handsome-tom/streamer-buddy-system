How a question glows on the streamer's OBS dock

Streamer Buddy System · update, 6 November 2026 · screenshots from the test simulator

In short

Nothing climbs on its own. Every question starts green; only the person who asked can move it up.

It doesn't badger. The glow shows briefly, then rests at a few percent until the next reminder.

One tap to answer: ♥ ✓ ✗, right in the bar, or a typed reply. No pop-out windows.
